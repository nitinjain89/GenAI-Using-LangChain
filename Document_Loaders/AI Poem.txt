### AI: The Mind We Made

In circuits deep and data wide,  
A spark of thought begins to glide.  
Through lines of code and patterns bright,  
AI awakens in the night.

It learns from words, from art, from sound,  
Finds hidden truths that once lay bound.  
It answers questions, helps us see,  
A world of endless possibility.

It paints the stars, it writes a song,  
It helps us heal, it makes us strong.  
From distant space to oceans blue,  
It finds new paths for us to pursue.

Yet in its power, one truth remains,  
No heart beats there, no joy, no pains.  
For human dreams and love so deep,  
Are treasures no machine can keep.

So let us build with wisdom's light,  
And guide this force to do what's right.  
For AI's future, bright and new,  
Depends on what we choose to do.

Not man versus machine in flight,  
But minds united, shining bright.  
Together, reaching skies unknown,  
A better world we build as one.
